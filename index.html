Petra is a powerful and versatile clipboard manager that allows copying and pasting a wide range of content, including text, images, links, console commands, and emojis. It features a robust search mechanism that lets you filter by copied item type or perform general searches, even by emoji.

Built for speed and efficiency, Petra offers complete keyboard navigation using q, w, ctrl+f, esc, and arrow keys, alongside visual conveniences like image previews and the ability to open links directly in your browser.

Customize your experience with a wide variety of themes and colors, choose to open the app on the left, right, or near your mouse position, and set your preferred keyboard shortcut.

With additional options to pin the window, start on system boot, and manually save, delete, or clear all items, Petra adapts entirely to your workflow.
